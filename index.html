      The device

      Where the water goes

      1. RiverThe free end of the inlet pipe is placed in the river.
      2. Foot valve and strainerKeeps out sand, leaves, and debris.
      3. Refill pump and screen filterDraws river water into the tank when it is low.
      4. Water tank2 float sensors tell when it is low or full.
      5. Irrigation pump and solenoid valveTurn on when the soil is dry and it is not raining.
      6. Drip lineReleases water near the roots of the crops.

      Main parts

      • ESP32 controller
      • LCD display
      • Soil-moisture sensors
      • Rain sensor
      • 2 float sensors
      • Refill pump

      • Drip line
      • Solar panel
      • Battery
      • Charge controller

      This is a user interface demonstration for the LCAIS thesis proposal. It runs on simulated data. The real system makes every decision on the ESP32 inside the device and does not need an internet connection.
